### Wow, Yours

Iɴ front of the flourishing bay[1] flowers sits Richard, who has newly been admitted to this widely known senior high school. With the persistent efforts paid, Richard ultimately fulfilled the goal imposed by his family.

Hearing the ring stricken, Richard carries his arranged schoolbag and walks through the corridor. Outside the gate are the tall trees on which green leaves are growing and colourful flowers are blooming, regardless of the fact that the cold of the winter hasn’t faded away.

‘So why all the people are accustomed to only appreciating the spring scenery, isn’t that silly?’ Richard talks to himself while getting through the gate.

Absorbed in the sunlight spots under the trees, Richard suddenly hits something on the ground. Richard finds that dozens of little bottles are scattered around a girl. Richard can recognize the yellow flowers in the bottles. There are three of the ones broken, with petals covering the glass pieces.

– ‘Oh, I’m sorry!’

‘It’s my fault. I’m sorry… sir …’

‘No, I … How are you now? Are you feeling well?’

‘Yes, I’m good … ’

‘I shall pay for the broken bottles.’

‘It doesn’t matter, I mean, they are beautiful and … shining.’

‘I got you. These are … bay flowers, right?’

‘ – Yeah, These are my favourite flowers, so I’ve made these bottles for sale to show their beauty,’

The girl replies, standing up while patting dirt off her white T-shirt. The girl starts picking the bottles up,

‘The bay flowers symbolize fresh vitality and the courage to face the challenges, a persistent attitude towards the tough life. Besides, they stands for success and honour, and, I believe that, that success belongs to everyone and one can be honoured at any time.

‘I do love it,so I picked them, dried them, bottled them, and, well, hoping someone will understand my heart.’

A gentle wind blows through, bringing the bay flowers up into the air, and the aroma fills Richard’s senses. After a moment of eye contact, Richard seems to be curious about the girl's life.

‘Well, I’d like to buy one. It seems that they have significant meanings to you … And, of course, I will pay for the broken ones.’

‘Really? I’m really glad that you like the flowers. But you shouldn’t, I mean, needn’t pay for them. You know, “It’s no use crying over spilt milk.” I never complain about whatever has happened. The best time to smell flowers is the present. Emm … you can take one for free – for you can understand me.’

‘Marvellous! It’s really a positive attitude towards life. Now that you’ve said that, I will take one and I will indeed cherish your warm heart. It’s really kind of you.’

Picked from the ground, the bottle turns warm in Richard's hands. The meanings hidden in the flowers seemly have made the bottle heavier.

Bᴀᴄᴋ from his office, Richard will be captured by the bay flowers in the table, being reminded of the tough girl and the living attitude which has been added to the word of wisdom. It shall be dinner time, Richard will come to the table and sit opposite to Elizabeth.

‘Honestly, I’ve changed a lot since a day 30 years ago.’

‘Hah, so you’ve kept the bottle for 30 years, right?’

In front of the bay flowers will stand a bird, twittering to celebrate the golden memory.
